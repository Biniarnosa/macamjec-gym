BACK + BICEP

Build a stronger back and develop powerful arms with focused pulling exercises.

BACK WORKOUT

BACK EXERCISES

01

LAT PULLDOWN

Focus: Lats and upper back

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Sit comfortably at the lat pulldown machine.
  2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width.
  3. Keep your chest lifted and back straight.
  4. Pull the bar toward your upper chest.
  5. Slowly return the bar upward.
  6. Repeat with controlled movement.
3–4 SETS 8–12 REPS
02

SEATED CABLE ROW

Focus: Middle back and lats

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Sit on the cable row machine.
  2. Grip the handle with both hands.
  3. Keep your back straight and chest up.
  4. Pull the handle toward your stomach.
  5. Squeeze your shoulder blades together.
  6. Slowly extend your arms back.
3 SETS 8–12 REPS
03

DUMBBELL ROW

Focus: Lats and upper back

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Place one hand and knee on a bench.
  2. Hold a dumbbell with your other hand.
  3. Keep your back straight.
  4. Pull the dumbbell toward your hip.
  5. Lower the dumbbell slowly.
  6. Repeat on both sides.
3 SETS 8–12 REPS

BICEP WORKOUT

BICEP EXERCISES

04

DUMBBELL CURL

Focus: Biceps

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Stand upright while holding dumbbells.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  3. Curl one or both dumbbells upward.
  4. Squeeze your biceps at the top.
  5. Slowly lower the dumbbells.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
05

HAMMER CURL

Focus: Biceps and forearms

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Hold the dumbbells with your palms facing each other.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  3. Curl the dumbbells upward.
  4. Squeeze your arms at the top.
  5. Lower the dumbbells slowly.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
