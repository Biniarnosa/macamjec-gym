CHEST + TRICEP

Build your chest and strengthen your triceps with this focused workout session.

CHEST WORKOUT

CHEST EXERCISES

01

BENCH PRESS

Focus: Chest, shoulders and triceps

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Lie flat on the bench with your feet firmly on the floor.
  2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width.
  3. Lift the bar from the rack and position it above your chest.
  4. Slowly lower the bar toward your chest.
  5. Push the bar upward until your arms are extended.
  6. Repeat for your target repetitions.
3–4 SETS 8–12 REPS
02

INCLINE DUMBBELL PRESS

Focus: Upper chest and shoulders

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Set the bench to an incline position.
  2. Sit down while holding a dumbbell in each hand.
  3. Position the dumbbells near your upper chest.
  4. Press both dumbbells upward.
  5. Slowly lower them back to the starting position.
  6. Repeat with controlled movement.
3 SETS 8–12 REPS
03

CHEST FLY

Focus: Chest isolation

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Lie on a flat bench holding dumbbells above your chest.
  2. Keep a slight bend in your elbows.
  3. Slowly lower the dumbbells outward.
  4. Stretch your chest without forcing the movement.
  5. Bring the dumbbells back together above your chest.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS

TRICEP WORKOUT

TRICEP EXERCISES

04

TRICEP PUSH DOWN

Focus: Triceps

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Stand in front of the cable machine.
  2. Grip the handle with both hands.
  3. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  4. Push the handle downward until your arms are extended.
  5. Slowly return to the starting position.
  6. Repeat while keeping your elbows stable.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
05

OVERHEAD TRICEP EXTENSION

Focus: Triceps

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Hold one dumbbell with both hands.
  2. Raise the dumbbell above your head.
  3. Keep your elbows pointing forward.
  4. Lower the dumbbell behind your head.
  5. Extend your arms back to the starting position.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
