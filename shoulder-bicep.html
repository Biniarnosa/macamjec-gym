STEP 03

SHOULDER + BICEP

Build stronger shoulders and develop your biceps using controlled dumbbell exercises.

SHOULDER WORKOUT

SHOULDER EXERCISES

01

SEATED DUMBBELL SHOULDER PRESS

Focus: Shoulders and triceps

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Sit upright on a bench with back support.
  2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder level.
  3. Keep your chest up and core tight.
  4. Press both dumbbells overhead.
  5. Slowly lower them back to shoulder level.
3 SETS 8–12 REPS
02

DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE

Focus: Side shoulders

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Stand upright holding a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Keep your arms slightly bent.
  3. Raise the dumbbells out to your sides.
  4. Lift until your arms are approximately shoulder level.
  5. Slowly lower the dumbbells.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
03

FRONT RAISE

Focus: Front shoulders

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Stand upright holding dumbbells in front of your thighs.
  2. Keep your arms slightly bent.
  3. Raise the dumbbells forward.
  4. Stop around shoulder height.
  5. Slowly lower the dumbbells.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS

DUMBBELL BICEP WORKOUT

BICEP EXERCISES

04

ALTERNATING DUMBBELL CURL

Focus: Biceps

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Stand upright holding a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  3. Curl one dumbbell toward your shoulder.
  4. Squeeze your bicep at the top.
  5. Lower it slowly and repeat with the other arm.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
05

HAMMER CURL

Focus: Biceps and forearms

HOW TO PERFORM

  1. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with palms facing inward.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  3. Curl the dumbbells upward.
  4. Pause briefly at the top.
  5. Slowly lower the dumbbells.
3 SETS 10–15 REPS
